How to play

Things pour into a jar. Watch them fall, spin the jar to get a better look, then guess how many there are before the clock runs out.

Daily — 5 fresh jars a day, up to 100 points each. Over/Under — more or fewer than the number shown? Keep the streak alive. Fill To — hold to pour until you think you've hit the target. Practice — endless jars.
